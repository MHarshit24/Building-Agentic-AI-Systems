Investment Policy Statement

Our firm follows strict investment guidelines:

Risk Tolerance Levels:
| Profile | Equity % | Fixed Income % | Maximum Drawdown |
|---------|----------|----------------|------------------|
| Conservative | 30 | 70 | -10% |
| Moderate | 60 | 40 | -20% |
| Aggressive | 80 | 20 | -30% |

Rebalancing Policy:
Portfolios are rebalanced quarterly or when allocations drift more than 5% from target levels.
We use tax-loss harvesting strategies when applicable.

Approved Asset Classes:
- Domestic equities (large-cap, mid-cap, small-cap)
- International equities (developed and emerging markets)
- Fixed income (government bonds, corporate bonds, municipal bonds)
- Alternative investments (REITs, commodities)
