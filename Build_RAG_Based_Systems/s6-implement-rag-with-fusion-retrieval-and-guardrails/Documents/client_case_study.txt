Sarah, age 45, had an overly conservative portfolio yielding only 2% annually.

Solution:

- New Allocation: 60% equities, 40% bonds
- Target Annual Return: 6-7%

Results (12 months):

- Portfolio Value: $541,000
- Volatility: Within acceptable range
