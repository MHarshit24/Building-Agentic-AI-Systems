HEALTHY VEGETARIAN BREAKFAST RECIPES

Breakfast is the most important meal of the day, and starting with a nutritious vegetarian meal sets you up for success. Here are some delicious and healthy vegetarian breakfast options:

OATMEAL WITH FRUITS AND NUTS
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 2 cups water or plant-based milk
- 1 banana, sliced
- 1/4 cup berries (blueberries, strawberries)
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- 1 tablespoon chopped almonds
- 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup

Cook oats according to package directions. Top with fruits, nuts, and seeds. This provides complex carbohydrates, fiber, and protein to keep you full until lunch.

VEGETARIAN SCRAMBLED EGGS WITH VEGETABLES
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup chopped bell peppers
- 1/4 cup chopped mushrooms
- 1/4 cup spinach
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

Scramble eggs with vegetables for a protein-rich breakfast. Eggs provide complete protein and essential amino acids. Vegetables add fiber, vitamins, and minerals.

GREEK YOGURT PARFAIT
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup granola
- 1/2 cup mixed berries
- 1 tablespoon honey

Layer yogurt, granola, and berries for a balanced breakfast. Greek yogurt is high in protein and probiotics, which support digestive health.

AVOCADO TOAST
- 2 slices whole grain bread
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Red pepper flakes
- Salt and pepper

Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Spread on toasted whole grain bread. Avocados provide healthy monounsaturated fats and fiber.

NUTRITIONAL BENEFITS
Vegetarian breakfasts can provide all essential nutrients when properly planned. Focus on:
- Complete proteins: eggs, Greek yogurt, quinoa
- Complex carbohydrates: whole grains, oats
- Healthy fats: avocados, nuts, seeds
- Fiber: fruits, vegetables, whole grains
- Vitamins and minerals: fresh produce
