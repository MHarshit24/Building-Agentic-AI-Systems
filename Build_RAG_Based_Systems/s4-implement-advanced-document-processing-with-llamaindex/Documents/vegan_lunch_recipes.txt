HEALTHY VEGAN LUNCH RECIPES

Vegan lunches can be nutritious, satisfying, and delicious. Here are some plant-based lunch options that provide complete nutrition:

QUINOA BUDDHA BOWL
- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- 1/2 cup chickpeas
- 1 cup roasted vegetables (sweet potato, broccoli, bell peppers)
- 1/4 avocado, sliced
- 2 tablespoons tahini dressing
- Fresh herbs (cilantro, parsley)

Quinoa provides complete protein with all essential amino acids. Chickpeas add additional protein and fiber. This bowl is rich in vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants.

LENTIL SOUP
- 1 cup red lentils
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 celery stalks, chopped
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- Salt and pepper

Lentils are an excellent source of plant-based protein (18g per cup) and fiber. This soup is filling, nutritious, and easy to prepare in advance.

VEGAN WRAP
- 1 large whole wheat tortilla
- 1/2 cup hummus
- 1 cup mixed greens
- 1/2 cup roasted vegetables
- 1/4 cup sprouts
- 1/4 avocado

Wraps are convenient and portable. Hummus provides protein and healthy fats. Load up on vegetables for fiber and nutrients.

CHICKPEA SALAD SANDWICH
- 1 can chickpeas, mashed
- 2 tablespoons vegan mayonnaise
- 1 stalk celery, chopped
- 1/4 cup red onion, diced
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Salt and pepper
- Whole grain bread

A vegan alternative to chicken salad. Chickpeas provide protein and fiber. This is a satisfying and protein-rich lunch option.

STIR-FRIED TOFU WITH VEGETABLES
- 8 oz firm tofu, cubed
- 2 cups mixed vegetables (broccoli, bell peppers, snap peas)
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon ginger, minced
- 1 clove garlic, minced

Tofu is a complete protein source. This dish provides protein, fiber, and various vitamins and minerals from vegetables.

NUTRITIONAL TIPS FOR VEGAN LUNCHES
- Include a protein source: legumes, tofu, tempeh, seitan
- Add healthy fats: avocado, nuts, seeds, olive oil
- Include complex carbohydrates: whole grains, sweet potatoes
- Load up on vegetables for fiber and micronutrients
- Consider B12 supplementation (not naturally found in plant foods)
- Ensure adequate iron intake (pair with vitamin C for absorption)

MEAL PREP TIPS
- Cook grains and legumes in bulk
- Prepare dressings and sauces ahead
- Roast vegetables in advance
- Store components separately to maintain freshness
- Assemble meals the night before or morning of
