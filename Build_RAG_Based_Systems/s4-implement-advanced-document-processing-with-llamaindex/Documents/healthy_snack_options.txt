HEALTHY SNACK OPTIONS FOR BETWEEN MEALS

Smart snacking can help maintain energy levels, prevent overeating at meals, and provide essential nutrients. Here are healthy snack options:

FRUIT AND NUT COMBINATIONS
- Apple slices with almond butter
- Banana with walnuts
- Berries with Greek yogurt
- Pear with cashews

Fruits provide natural sugars for quick energy, while nuts add protein and healthy fats to keep you satisfied.

VEGETABLE SNACKS
- Carrot sticks with hummus
- Celery with peanut butter
- Cucumber slices with tzatziki
- Bell pepper strips with guacamole

Vegetables are low in calories and high in fiber, vitamins, and minerals. Pairing with protein or healthy fats increases satiety.

PROTEIN-RICH SNACKS
- Hard-boiled eggs
- Greek yogurt with berries
- Cottage cheese with fruit
- Turkey or chicken slices
- Edamame

Protein snacks help maintain muscle mass, keep you full, and stabilize blood sugar levels.

WHOLE GRAIN SNACKS
- Whole grain crackers with cheese
- Rice cakes with avocado
- Oatmeal with nuts and fruit
- Whole grain toast with nut butter

Complex carbohydrates provide sustained energy and fiber for digestive health.

NUT AND SEED MIXES
- Trail mix (almonds, walnuts, dried fruit)
- Pumpkin seeds
- Sunflower seeds
- Chia seed pudding

Nuts and seeds provide healthy fats, protein, and various micronutrients. Portion control is important due to high calorie density.

DAIRY-BASED SNACKS
- Greek yogurt parfait
- String cheese with whole grain crackers
- Cottage cheese with fruit
- Kefir smoothie

Dairy provides protein and calcium. Choose low-sugar options when possible.

SNACK TIMING
- Eat snacks 2-3 hours after meals
- Have a snack if more than 4-5 hours between meals
- Pre-workout: 30-60 minutes before exercise
- Post-workout: within 30 minutes after exercise

PORTION GUIDELINES
- Keep snacks between 150-250 calories
- Include protein (5-10g) and fiber (3-5g)
- Avoid mindless snacking
- Plan snacks ahead of time

SNACKS TO LIMIT
- Processed chips and crackers
- Sugary granola bars
- Candy and sweets
- Soda and sugary drinks
- High-sugar yogurts

These provide empty calories and can lead to energy crashes and weight gain.

HYDRATION
- Drink water with snacks
- Herbal teas are good low-calorie options
- Avoid sugary beverages
- Stay hydrated throughout the day
