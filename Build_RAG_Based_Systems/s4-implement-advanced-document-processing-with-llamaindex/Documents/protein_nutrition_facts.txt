PROTEIN NUTRITION FACTS AND GUIDELINES

Protein is an essential macronutrient that plays a crucial role in building and repairing tissues, supporting immune function, and maintaining muscle mass. Understanding protein needs and sources is important for a balanced diet.

DAILY PROTEIN REQUIREMENTS
- Average adult: 0.8 grams per kilogram of body weight
- Active individuals: 1.2-1.7 grams per kilogram
- Athletes: 1.6-2.2 grams per kilogram
- Older adults: 1.0-1.2 grams per kilogram

For example, a 70kg (154lb) person needs approximately 56 grams of protein per day for basic requirements, or 84-112 grams for active individuals.

COMPLETE VS INCOMPLETE PROTEINS
Complete proteins contain all nine essential amino acids:
- Animal sources: meat, poultry, fish, eggs, dairy
- Plant sources: quinoa, soy, chia seeds

Incomplete proteins lack one or more essential amino acids:
- Most plant sources: beans, rice, nuts, vegetables
- Can be combined to form complete proteins (e.g., rice and beans)

HIGH PROTEIN FOOD SOURCES

Animal Sources:
- Chicken breast (31g per 100g)
- Salmon (25g per 100g)
- Eggs (6g per large egg)
- Greek yogurt (10g per 100g)
- Lean beef (26g per 100g)

Plant Sources:
- Lentils (9g per 100g cooked)
- Chickpeas (8g per 100g cooked)
- Tofu (8g per 100g)
- Quinoa (4g per 100g cooked)
- Almonds (21g per 100g)
- Chia seeds (17g per 100g)

PROTEIN DISTRIBUTION
- Distribute protein intake across meals
- Aim for 20-30 grams per meal
- Include protein in snacks
- Consume protein within 2 hours after exercise for muscle recovery

BENEFITS OF ADEQUATE PROTEIN
- Supports muscle growth and maintenance
- Aids in weight management (increases satiety)
- Supports bone health
- Helps maintain healthy hair, skin, and nails
- Supports immune function
- Aids in wound healing

PROTEIN AND WEIGHT MANAGEMENT
- High-protein diets can increase metabolism
- Protein increases feelings of fullness
- Helps preserve muscle mass during weight loss
- Requires more energy to digest (thermic effect)

SIGNS OF PROTEIN DEFICIENCY
- Muscle weakness or loss
- Fatigue
- Slow wound healing
- Hair loss
- Brittle nails
- Frequent infections

PROTEIN SUPPLEMENTS
- Whey protein: fast-digesting, good for post-workout
- Casein protein: slow-digesting, good before bed
- Plant-based proteins: suitable for vegetarians/vegans
- Should supplement, not replace, whole food sources
