DIABETIC MEAL PLANNING GUIDELINES

Managing diabetes through diet requires careful attention to carbohydrate intake, meal timing, and food choices. Here are essential guidelines for diabetic meal planning:

CARBOHYDRATE MANAGEMENT
- Monitor total carbohydrates per meal (typically 45-60 grams)
- Choose complex carbohydrates over simple sugars
- Spread carbohydrates evenly throughout the day
- Count carbohydrates to maintain blood sugar control

RECOMMENDED FOODS
- Non-starchy vegetables: broccoli, spinach, bell peppers, cauliflower
- Lean proteins: chicken, fish, tofu, legumes
- Whole grains: brown rice, quinoa, whole wheat bread
- Healthy fats: olive oil, avocados, nuts
- Low-glycemic fruits: berries, apples, pears

FOODS TO LIMIT OR AVOID
- Refined sugars: candy, soda, desserts
- White bread and white rice
- Processed foods high in sodium
- Sugary beverages
- High-fat processed meats

MEAL TIMING
- Eat meals at consistent times each day
- Don't skip meals
- Space meals 4-5 hours apart
- Include snacks if needed to prevent low blood sugar

SAMPLE MEAL PLAN

Breakfast (45g carbs):
- 1 slice whole grain toast
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup berries
- 1 cup unsweetened coffee

Lunch (50g carbs):
- Grilled chicken salad with mixed vegetables
- 1/2 cup quinoa
- 1 tablespoon olive oil dressing
- 1 small apple

Dinner (45g carbs):
- Baked salmon (4 oz)
- 1 cup steamed broccoli
- 1/2 cup brown rice
- Mixed green salad

Snacks (15g carbs each):
- 1/4 cup almonds
- 1 small pear
- Greek yogurt (unsweetened)

BLOOD SUGAR MONITORING
- Check blood sugar before meals
- Monitor 2 hours after meals
- Keep a food and blood sugar log
- Work with healthcare provider to adjust meal plan

HYDRATION
- Drink water throughout the day
- Limit sugary drinks
- Avoid alcohol or consume in moderation with food
