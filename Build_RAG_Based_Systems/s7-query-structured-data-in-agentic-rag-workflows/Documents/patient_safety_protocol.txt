Patient Safety and Readmission Prevention
High-Risk Factors for Readmission:
    1. Age 65+ (increased monitoring required)
    2. Length of stay >7 days (higher complexity)
    3. Emergency admissions (often unplanned)
Preventive Measures:
    • Post-discharge follow-up within 48 hours for high-risk patients
    • Care transition coordinators for patients 65+
    • Patient education before discharge
    • Medication reconciliation
Regulatory Compliance: CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid) penalizes hospitals with readmission rates exceeding national benchmarks by >3%.
