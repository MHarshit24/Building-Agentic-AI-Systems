Healthcare Quality Metrics - 2025 Standards
Readmission Rate Targets:
    • Cardiology: Target <12% (industry benchmark: 15%)
    • Orthopedics: Target <8% (industry benchmark: 10%)
    • Emergency: Target <5% (industry benchmark: 7%)
    • Pediatrics: Target <3% (industry benchmark: 4%)
Bed Utilization Targets: Optimal utilization range is 75-85%. Below 75% indicates underutilization; above 85% risks overcrowding and reduced care quality.
Average Length of Stay (ALOS) Goals:
    • Cardiology: 4.5 days
    • Orthopedics: 6.0 days
    • Emergency: 1.5 days
    • Pediatrics: 3.0 days
