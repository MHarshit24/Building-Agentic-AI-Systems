Capacity Management Best Practices
Utilization Rate Interpretation:
    • <70%: Significant underutilization, financial inefficiency
    • 70-75%: Below optimal, capacity for growth
    • 75-85%: Optimal range (TARGET)
    • 85-90%: High utilization, monitor for strain
    • >90%: Overcrowding risk, quality concerns
Department-Specific Considerations: Emergency Department: Higher variability expected, focus on surge capacity readiness rather than consistent utilization.
Elective Surgery Departments (e.g., Orthopedics): Should maintain 80-85% utilization for maximum efficiency while preserving emergency capacity.
